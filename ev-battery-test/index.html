What you get

What your certificate shows.

  • SOHState of Health: the battery's current capacity as a percentage of new
  • KWHUsable capacity: measured against the battery's rated figure
  • RESResult: a plain-English rating of the battery's health
  • IDAssessment ID: a unique ID that can be checked online

A CellSure certificate gives you an objective result with no stake in the sale. We never buy or sell cars. It's evidence you can negotiate with, or walk away on.
