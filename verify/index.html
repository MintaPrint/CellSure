Check a certificate

Enter the assessment ID and registration from the certificate. If they match our records, you will see the test result.

The assessment ID is 8 characters. It is at the top of every certificate, for example 68675F31.

Each certificate only shows when both the ID and the registration are entered. We do not publish a list of tested cars.
